Minnow Run

Tap to swim. Weave plants, dodge fish!

A one-life arcade swim for Android. Coming soon to Google Play.

Tap to rise, let go to sink, and thread your minnow through the weeds and past the bigger fish for as long as you can. Every run starts at dawn and swims into night.

  • One tap, one life. Runs are short and the retry is instant.
  • Graze the big fish for bonus points, if you dare.
  • Nine skins to unlock, earned by score and by nerve.
  • Three-letter arcade profiles and a local leaderboard, so everyone on the phone keeps their own best.
  • Original chiptune music and sound, each with its own mute.
  • No account, no sign-in, nothing to buy. Free, with an occasional ad between runs. Made for families: see the privacy policy.

Made by a dad, tested by kids. Fish sticks!
