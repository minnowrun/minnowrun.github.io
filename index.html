How it plays

Easy to pick up, hard to put down. Runs are short, and every one starts fresh.

One tap, one life

Tap to swim up; stop tapping and you sink. Bump a plant, a rock, the surface or the bottom, or get caught by a big fish, and the run is over. Try again in a tap.

Dodge or graze

Bass, pike and bluegill cruise through. Dodge them, or swim close past one without touching it for a graze worth 2 bonus points.

Nine fish to swim as

Start as the classic Minnow and earn eight more, from Ember and Frostbite to Mecha-Minnow and Rainbow, by beating your best or grazing fish.

Day into night

Every run starts in daylight. The farther you swim, the pond turns to dusk, then night, then dawn.

Everyone keeps their best

Pick three arcade initials and the whole family can share one phone, each with their own best score and skins, and one leaderboard for bragging rights.

Original chiptune

Two original chiptune tunes and retro sound effects, with a separate mute for the music and for the sound.

Made for families

Made by a dad, tested by kids. Fish sticks!

  • No account, nothing to sign up for. Open it and swim.
  • Your game stays on your phone. Initials, scores and skins are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Kid-safe ads, and not many. At most one full-screen ad every three runs, always requested as child-directed, non-personalized and rated G.
  • Nothing to buy. No in-app purchases, no chat, no social features.
